An optional email subscription for developers using Axyra Sheets. No account is
created, and nothing on this site or in the Agent Skill requires it.

Subscribers receive:

- New or updated Agent Skills, with a short note on what changed and who benefits.
- Examples and sample projects, plus migration and compatibility guidance.
- Evaluation tips and links to the current license options.

Need help now rather than updates? [Request evaluation help]({{ '/axyra/evaluation-help' | relative_url }}?src=updates_page)
or [discuss pricing or deployment]({{ '/axyra/contact-sales' | relative_url }}?src=updates_page).
Neither requires a subscription.
